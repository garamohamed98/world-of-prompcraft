The Core Concept

What if every game had just one button — your keyboard?

World of Promptcraft is a 3D multiplayer RPG where the entire interface is a text prompt. There are no action bars, no dialog trees, no skill menus. You type what you want to do — and the game's AI makes it real.

Every NPC is a fully autonomous AI agent with its own memory, personality, and mood. They remember your name, your past actions, and how you treated them. Befriend a merchant and she'll give you discounts. Insult a guard and he'll hold a grudge.

Combat rewards creativity — a plain "I attack" deals glancing damage, while a vivid "I vault off the crumbling wall and drive my blazing blade into the dragon's eye" can be devastating.

How to Play

Your Keyboard Is All You Need

1

Choose Your Hero

Pick a name, race (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc), and faction (Alliance or Horde). You spawn in a vast fantasy world.

2

Explore the World

Walk through lush forests, volcanic badlands, frozen tundras, and ancient ruins. Every step reveals something new.

3

Find an NPC

Click any character you meet. Instead of a dialog tree, you get a text box. This is your only interface.

4

Type Anything

Say hello, ask for a quest, offer a trade, start a fight, tell a joke, propose a deal, or try to pickpocket them. The AI responds instantly.

5

Watch the World React

Damage numbers fly, particle effects ignite, NPCs emote, weather changes, music swells — all driven by your words.
